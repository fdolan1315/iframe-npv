The current third (mono) face and 12.5–14px text go.

One rule for every screen

  • One hero figure, set on the page
  • Up to five notes on the figure
  • Under 60 words of running text
  • Visible source line beneath
  • Tables one click away
Media

Make three short films from the same drawings, and almost no generated imagery

Films, code-rendered

  • Gain vs loss · 30 s
  • The tremor loop · 40 s
  • The odyssey / the leaky pipeline · 30 s, optional

Captions on screen, no numbers baked in, under 5 MB each. Remotion and Hyperframes both run in this environment.

Stills, generated

  • Older hands steadying a cup
  • A hand signing a form
  • A parent's hand holding an infant's
  • A seizure diary on a counter

Six to eight, documentary style, no faces, captioned "Illustrative image". Blocked today: the image service has 0 credits.

Never generate

  • Ion channels, neurons, brain anatomy
  • Scans, EEG, charts, maps
  • Pills, packaging, any drug product
  • Faces, children, a seizure
  • Anything containing text

Mechanisms are drawn in code so they stay correct.

Your call

Five decisions before the build

1
May mechanism schematics be drawn, given the kit bans "molecule diagrams"?
Recommend yes: flat, labelled, slate line drawings are instruction, not brand imagery.
2
May table cells and compact metadata drop to 14px?
Recommend yes. At the kit's 16–18px, tables run 30–40% longer.
3
Is documentary photography of hands and homes right for BIOS?
Optional. The app works without it; it needs image credits either way.
4
Light theme only, with the product title as plain text beside the wordmark?
Recommend yes to both. Cobalt on slate fails text contrast.
5
Who signs off the science in the mechanism figures?
A neurologist or medical lead should review before anything is shown externally.
