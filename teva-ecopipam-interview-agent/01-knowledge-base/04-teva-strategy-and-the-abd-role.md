# Knowledge Base — Teva, "Pivot to Growth," and the ABD Role

> **Purpose:** the company, its strategy, its neuroscience portfolio, its values, and the actual job-description language for the ABD and the roles it manages — plus what to expect from Teva's interview process. Reference doc for the agents and candidate.

## 0. Why this role exists (the thread through everything)

This ABD job is a **direct manifestation of Teva's corporate strategy.** In 2026 Teva **acquired Emalex Biosciences** (~$700M upfront + up to $200M milestones + royalties; ~$900M total; closed June 10, 2026) specifically to bring **ecopipam** — an NDA-ready, first-in-class D1 antagonist for pediatric TS — into its neuroscience pipeline. Teva frames it as adding an *"NDA-ready, first-in-class therapy to [the] neuroscience pipeline"* that is *"accelerating Teva's Pivot to Growth strategy."*

**Interview implication:** connect "why I want this role" to **Pivot to Growth**, **neuroscience as a core therapeutic area**, and **leveraging Teva's existing Austedo/Uzedy field infrastructure**. Ecopipam is a proof point that Teva can buy late-stage neuroscience assets and commercialize them through the CNS engine it already built. This was **Teva's first M&A in years** — a signal of conviction.

## 1. Company overview

- **What it is:** Teva Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. — Israeli-founded multinational, historically the **world's largest generics maker**, now repositioning as *"a leading innovative biopharmaceutical company enabled by a world-class generics business"* (memorize this phrasing).
- **Heritage:** roots in **1901, Jerusalem** ("Teva" is Hebrew for "nature"). Built into a global generics powerhouse under long-time CEO **Eli Hurvitz**; scaled via **Barr (2008), Cephalon (2011), Actavis Generics (2016)** — the last cemented #1 in generics but loaded significant debt.
- **HQ:** global HQ **Petah Tikva, Israel**; **US commercial operations in Parsippany, New Jersey** (relevant to this role).
- **Scale:** NYSE: **TEVA**; revenue **~$16.5B (FY2024) → ~$17.3B (FY2025)**, 2026 guide ~$16.4–16.8B; **~37,000 employees**; ~3,600 medicines; nearly **200 million people in ~60 markets**.
- **Leadership:** President & CEO **Richard Francis** (since Jan 1, 2023; ex-Sandoz). Architect of the turnaround — FY2025 was the **third consecutive year of growth**.

## 2. "Pivot to Growth" — the strategic vocabulary of the interview

Launched **May 18, 2023** under Francis. Reframes Teva from a debt-laden generics company into a **branded/innovative-medicines company that still runs a strong generics engine.** Know the **four pillars**:

1. **Deliver on our growth engines** — maximize the innovative commercial portfolio + biosimilars. Home of the **neuroscience brands (Austedo/Austedo XR, Uzedy, Ajovy).**
2. **Step up innovation** — advance the pipeline toward **first-in-class/best-in-class** assets, concentrated in **neuroscience, immunology, immuno-oncology.** *(Ecopipam is a textbook "step up innovation" acquisition.)*
3. **Sustain our generics powerhouse** — the cash-generating foundation.
4. **Focus the business** — disciplined capital allocation, deleveraging, portfolio focus.

**Financial narrative:** innovative portfolio grown to ~$2.3B (~30% over two years); public target of **~$4–5B in innovative-medicine sales by ~2027–2030**, with **neuroscience as the spearhead.**

**Best "Why Teva?" answer:** name Pivot to Growth and the four pillars, and position the ABD job as **executing Pillar 1 (deliver on growth engines) while launching the fruits of Pillar 2 (step up innovation) — i.e., ecopipam.**

## 3. Teva's neuroscience portfolio — and how ecopipam fits

Teva has built a **US neuroscience/psychiatry commercial organization** calling on neurologists, psychiatrists, and movement-disorder specialists. Ecopipam is designed to **leverage this infrastructure**, not build anew.

- **Austedo® / Austedo XR® (deutetrabenazine)** — flagship growth driver; first VMAT2 inhibitor for **tardive dyskinesia** and **Huntington's chorea**. XR is once-daily (2023). **~$1.6B in 2024**; guided **≥$2.5B by 2027**. A market-development story (large under-diagnosed/under-treated TD population).
- **Uzedy® (risperidone ER injectable)** — long-acting subcutaneous (SteadyTeq/Medincell) for **schizophrenia**, monthly/bi-monthly (2023). Psychiatry call point; brings **injectable, buy-and-bill, specialty-pharmacy, HUB/reimbursement** complexity — the same access muscles a pediatric orphan launch needs.
- **Ajovy® (fremanezumab)** — anti-CGRP for **migraine**; now the **first anti-CGRP approved for pediatric episodic migraine (6–17)** — proof Teva can expand neuroscience brands into **pediatric** populations (directly relevant to a pediatric TS launch).

**The thesis to articulate:** TS is a **pediatric neuro-psychiatric movement disorder** sitting at the intersection of Teva's two CNS franchises. Same call points (child neurologists, pediatric psychiatrists, movement-disorder specialists), same commercial muscles (specialty/orphan launch, KOL development, HUB/access, buy-and-bill from Uzedy, pediatric playbook from Ajovy, market-development from Austedo). Ecopipam's **first-in-class D1 mechanism** is the clinical selling story.

## 5. Job-description language — mirror these exact phrases

### A) Area Business Director (CNS) — the target role (second-line)
Teva runs live ABD (CNS) postings by geography (e.g., Pacific, Midwest). Captured language:
- **Positioning:** ABDs are *"second-line sales leaders responsible for creating a high-performance culture and bringing a general management approach to the business within the psychiatry and neuroscience spaces."*
- **Core mandate:** *"identify performance challenges and proactively engage their teams and cross-functional partners to build and implement practical solutions."*
- **Responsibilities (verbatim/near-verbatim):**
  - *"Developing and coaching Managers and Senior Managers on the strategic sales vision and executional expectations for the teams, ensuring alignment to organizational strategy."* (An ABD **manages first-line District/Regional Sales Managers — coaching the coaches.**)
  - *"Accelerating sales performance within the assigned market… and the development of people."*
  - *"Leading and overseeing all market initiatives to completion, leveraging and aligning internal Teva commercial resources to… optimize the customer experience."*
  - *"Developing, implementing, and executing strategic plans to achieve revenue targets and market share goals."*
  - *"Analyzing market trends, competitor activities, and customer feedback to make informed decisions."*
- **Qualifications:** **≥10 years biotech/pharma** (CNS/Psychiatry/Neuroscience/LAI strongly preferred); **≥5 years regional sales/people-management**; *"a successful track record of achieving sales goals and building, coaching, and developing effective teams is required."* Live near a major airport; travel.
- **Comp (as posted):** roughly **$198,800–$280,000** base by geography.
- **Buzzwords to internalize:** *high-performance culture; general management approach; coaching Managers and Senior Managers; strategic sales vision; executional expectations; alignment to organizational strategy; accelerating sales performance; leveraging/aligning internal commercial resources; optimize the customer experience; developing people.*

### B) First-line managers the ABD oversees — "Neuroscience / NeuroPsych / NeuroHealth Regional Sales Manager"
The people an ABD develops. Posted responsibilities:
- Field performance of the **LAI for schizophrenia (Uzedy)** plus **movement-disorder neurology products (Austedo).**
- *"Identifying, recruiting, coaching, and retaining top talent."*
- Developing expertise on **regional focus areas including settings of care and payer opportunities.**
- *"Developing effective business plans to meet and exceed regional sales goals"*; *"coaching teams on execution of defined call plans."*
- **Preferred:** launching in the **psychiatric space** across settings; **injectable/in-office** experience; **rare-disease** experience with **specialty pharmacies, buy-and-bill, HUB services**; **reimbursement and pull-through** fluency; CMHC/specialty-pharmacy/private-practice settings.
- Comp example ~$160K–$200K base.

### C) First-line rep — "Neuroscience Sales Specialist"
- *"Strategic, results-driven professional responsible for significant sales growth… through compliant, ethical solutions."* Account-based selling; *"strong total office relationships"*; cross-functional collaboration; territory plans from market data; HCP product education.
- **Quals:** ~3–5 yrs pharma/medical sales (neuroscience preferred); communicate complex science; proven target attainment. Comp ~$88K–$170K.

### D) What second-line pharma sales managers require generally
- **Lead through leaders** — coach/develop *first-line managers*, build manager-of-managers bench and succession.
- Own **P&L / general-management thinking** — forecasting, resource allocation, budget, incentive comp.
- Drive **launch excellence** — account/segment strategy, KOL and IDN/health-system engagement, market access and pull-through, speed-to-peak.
- Build a **high-performance, compliant culture** — hire top talent, manage performance, ensure PhRMA-code/regulatory compliance in a promotionally sensitive CNS space.
- Be the **connective tissue** translating corporate strategy into field execution across marketing, market access, medical, analytics.

## 6. Interview style — what to expect

**Process:** recruiter screen → possible self-recorded video interview → hiring-manager interview → panel/team → **role-related assessment** (for sales leadership, expect a **business case, mock coaching scenario, or territory/launch-plan presentation**). Timelines run several weeks.

**Method — behavioral / competency-based (the key):** Teva explicitly states it *"believes in behavioral interviews and will ask you to tell them about a time you handled certain situations."* Big pharma (Teva in this mold) uses **DDI Targeted Selection®** and the **STAR method** (DDI created STAR). Every candidate is scored against the **same competency set**; interviewers probe for **what you personally did** (not hypotheticals) and press for **quantified results**.

**Likely competencies to prep STAR stories for:**
- Turning around an underperforming manager/district (coaching the coaches).
- A launch you led and how you drove **speed-to-peak** (directly relevant to ecopipam).
- Building a **high-performance culture** / hiring and developing top talent.
- Aligning **cross-functional partners** (marketing, market access, medical) to solve a business problem.
- Navigating a **compliance-sensitive** situation (salient given Teva's history and CNS promotion).
- Translating **corporate strategy into field execution** and hitting the number.

**Preparation advice:**
- Come armed with **quantified results** — share gains, quota attainment, rankings, launch metrics, retention, promotions of your direct reports.
- **Speak Teva's dialect** — "Pivot to Growth," "growth engines," "high-performance culture," "general management approach," "coaching Managers and Senior Managers," "all in for better health," plus value names like "Accountability" and "Getting it Done."
- **Tie your candidacy to ecopipam + the CNS franchise** — you'd launch a first-in-class D1 antagonist for **pediatric** TS **through the same field force** carrying Austedo/Uzedy, and you grasp specialty-launch complexity (orphan patient-finding, HUB/access, buy-and-bill, pediatric prescriber dynamics, KOL development).
