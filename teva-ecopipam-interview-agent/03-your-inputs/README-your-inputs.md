# Your Inputs — the three things only you can supply

The knowledge base and agent prompts are built. To make the mock interviews genuinely useful, add **your own material**. The agent can coach you on market knowledge from the knowledge base, but it can only coach you on *your* stories if you give them to it.

Add these three files to the agent's knowledge base alongside the `01-knowledge-base` documents:

1. **`your-resume.md`** (or PDF) — paste or upload your current resume. Lets the Interviewer ask about your actual roles and the Coach reference your real experience.

2. **`your-star-story-bank.md`** — use the template in this folder. This is the highest-leverage input: 8–12 pre-built STAR stories mapped to the competencies an ABD interview will hit. Even drafting them is great prep; having the agent drill you on them is better.

3. **`the-job-description.md`** — paste the actual Teva ABD (CNS) job posting you're interviewing for, once you have it. The generic ABD language is already captured in knowledge-base doc 04, but the real posting may have specifics (region, products, exact competencies) worth training on.

**Privacy note:** these files contain your personal information. They live in your repo and your ElevenLabs account. Keep the knowledge base under the non-enterprise cap (~300k characters / ~50–60k words total across all docs) — you're nowhere near it, so add freely.
