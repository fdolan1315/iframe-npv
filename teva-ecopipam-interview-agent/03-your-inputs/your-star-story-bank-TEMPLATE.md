# Your STAR Story Bank — Template

> Fill in 8–12 stories. Aim to cover every competency below at least once. Keep each story to what you could say in **90 seconds to 2 minutes**.
> **Sequencing tip (the hidden unlock):** write the **Headline/Result first** — the one-line outcome you'd lead with — then Situation, Task, Action, Result. In the interview you deliver them roughly in that order so the interviewer always knows where the story is going.

For each story, fill in:
- **Headline (lead with this):** the one-sentence outcome/result.
- **S — Situation:** brief context (who, where, when). Keep it short.
- **T — Task:** *your specific* responsibility / what you owned.
- **A — Action:** the specific steps **you** took (use "I", not "we"). For a second-line role, show how you **led through your managers**.
- **R — Result:** quantified outcome (%, ranking, quota, headcount, retention, timeline, $).
- **Learned / would do differently:** one crisp line (interviewers often probe this).

## Competency checklist (map your stories to these)

Priority competencies for a second-line ABD role — make sure each is covered:

- [ ] **Leading through leaders** — coaching a manager to handle something rather than stepping in yourself
- [ ] **Turnaround** — inherited an underperforming region/district/manager and fixed it
- [ ] **Talent development** — someone you coached who got promoted or grew significantly
- [ ] **Tough people decision** — an underperforming manager you had to act on
- [ ] **Launch / build** — launched a product or built a team/territory from scratch
- [ ] **Change leadership** — led a team through a reorg, launch delay, or strategy shift
- [ ] **Compliance / integrity** — handled an off-label or policy issue; chose right over the number
- [ ] **Business acumen** — used data to reallocate people/budget/targeting; built a region business plan
- [ ] **Learning from failure** — a strategy that underdelivered and what you learned
- [ ] **Cross-functional influence** — moved marketing / market access / medical without authority
- [ ] **Account leadership** — a health system / IDN / center of excellence relationship or complex access win
- [ ] **Resilience / self-awareness** — toughest year; feedback that changed you
- [ ] **Motivation / fit** — why Teva, why this role (tie to Pivot to Growth + ecopipam)

### Two "must-have" stories for THIS role
Because ecopipam is a **pre-approval pediatric orphan launch**, prepare these two with extra care:

**A. The launch story.** A time you launched a product or built something from nothing — ideally showing how you sequenced the first 6–12 months, made targeting/segmentation calls, and drove speed-to-peak. This is the story the ABD role is really testing.

**B. The pre-approval compliance story.** A time you kept an eager team inside the promotional/regulatory lines — especially relevant because ecopipam isn't approved yet, so the first year is disease-state education only, no product promotion. If you don't have an exact pre-approval example, use the closest compliance judgment call you've made and be ready to reason about the pre-approval scenario.
